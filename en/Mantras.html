Daily Essential Mantras

Krishna Pranam Radha Pranam Vaishnava Pranam Tulasi Pranam Tulasi Snana Guru Pranam Pancha-Tattva Pranam Gaura Pranam Hare Krishna Maha-Mantra Narasimha Pranam Damodara Prayer Vrindavan Pranam

Krishna Pranam

कृष्णाय वासुदेवाय हरये परमात्मने । प्रणतः क्लेशनाशाय गोविन्दाय नमो नमः ॥

Krishnaya Vasudevaya Haraye Paramatmane | Pranatah Kleshanashaya Govindaya Namo Namah ||

Obeisances again and again to Krishna, the son of Vasudeva, the Supreme Lord, Govinda, who destroys the sufferings of those who surrender unto Him.

Radha Pranam

तप्तकाञ्चन गौराङ्गी राधे वृन्दावनेश्वरी । वृषभानुसुते देवि प्रणमामि हरिप्रिये ॥

Taptakanchana Gaurangi Radhe Vrindavaneshvari | Vrishabhanusute Devi Pranamami Haripriye ||

O Radha, whose complexion is like molten gold, O Queen of Vrindavan, daughter of Vrishabhanu, beloved of Lord Hari, I offer my respectful obeisances unto You.

Vaishnava Pranam

वाञ्छा-कल्पतरुभ्यश्च कृपा-सिन्धुभ्य एव च । पतितानां पावनेभ्यो वैष्णवेभ्यो नमो नमः ॥

Vancha-kalpa-tarubhyash cha Kripa-sindhubhya eva cha | Patitanam Pavanebhyo Vaishnavebhyo Namo Namah ||

I offer my respectful obeisances to the Vaishnavas, who are like desire trees and oceans of mercy, and who purify the fallen souls.

Tulasi Pranam

वृन्दायै तुलसीदेव्यै प्रियायै केशवस्य च । विष्णु-भक्ति-प्रदे देवि सत्यवत्यै नमो नमः ॥

Vrindayai Tulasi-devyai Priyayai Keshavasya Cha | Vishnu-bhakti-prade Devi Satyavatyai Namo Namah ||

Obeisances again and again to Vrinda Devi, Tulasi Devi, the beloved of Lord Keshava, who bestows devotion to Lord Vishnu.

Tulasi Snana

गोविन्द-वल्लभां देवीं भक्त-चैतन्य-कारिणीम् । स्नापयामि जगद्धात्रीं विष्णु-भक्ति-प्रदायिनीम् ॥

Govinda-vallabham Devim Bhakta-chaitanya-karinim | Snapayami Jagad-dhatrim Vishnu-bhakti-pradayinim ||

I bathe Tulasi Devi, the beloved of Govinda, who awakens devotion in the devotees and bestows devotion to Lord Vishnu.

Guru Pranam

अज्ञान-तिमिरान्धस्य ज्ञानाञ्जन-शलाकया । चक्षुरुन्मीलितं येन तस्मै श्री-गुरवे नमः ॥

Ajnana-timirandhasya Jnananjana-shalakaya | Chakshur Unmilitam Yena Tasmai Shri-Gurave Namah ||

I offer my respectful obeisances unto my spiritual master, who has opened my eyes, blinded by the darkness of ignorance, with the torchlight of knowledge.

Pancha-Tattva Pranam

श्री-कृष्ण-चैतन्य प्रभु नित्यानन्द । श्री-अद्वैत गदाधर श्रीवासादि गौर-भक्त-वृन्द ॥

Shri Krishna Chaitanya Prabhu Nityananda | Shri Advaita Gadadhara Shrivasadi Gaura-bhakta-vrinda ||

I offer my obeisances to Shri Krishna Chaitanya, Prabhu Nityananda, Shri Advaita, Gadadhara, Shrivasa, and all the devotees of Lord Gauranga.

Gaura Pranam

नमो महा-वदान्याय कृष्ण-प्रेम-प्रदाय ते । कृष्णाय कृष्ण-चैतन्य-नाम्ने गौरत्विषे नमः ॥

Namo Maha-vadanyaya Krishna-prema-pradaya Te | Krishnaya Krishna-Chaitanya-Namne Gaura-tvishe Namah ||

Obeisances to the most magnanimous Lord, who freely bestows pure love of Krishna. Obeisances to Lord Krishna Chaitanya, who has assumed a golden complexion.

Hare Krishna Maha-Mantra

हरे कृष्ण हरे कृष्ण कृष्ण कृष्ण हरे हरे । हरे राम हरे राम राम राम हरे हरे ॥

Hare Krishna Hare Krishna Krishna Krishna Hare Hare | Hare Rama Hare Rama Rama Rama Hare Hare ||

O Lord, O Divine Energy of the Lord, please engage me in Your loving devotional service.

Narasimha Pranam

नमस्ते नरसिंहाय प्रह्लादाह्लाद-दायिने । हिरण्यकशिपोर्वक्षः-शिला-टङ्क-नखालये ॥

Namaste Narasimhaya Prahladahlada-dayine | Hiranyakashipor Vakshah-shila-tanka-nakhalaye ||

Obeisances to Lord Narasimha, who gives joy to Prahlada and whose nails are like chisels that tore apart the stone-like chest of Hiranyakashipu.

Damodara Prayer

नमामीश्वरं सच्चिदानन्दरूपं लसत्कुण्डलं गोकुले भ्राजमानम् ।

Namamishvaram Sach-chid-ananda-rupam Lasat-kundalam Gokule Bhrajamanam

I offer my obeisances to the Supreme Lord, whose form is eternal, full of knowledge and bliss, and who shines beautifully in Gokula with His swinging earrings.

Vrindavan Pranam

वृन्दावनं परित्यज्य पदमेकं न गच्छति ।

Vrindavanam parityajya padam ekam na gachchhati.

Lord Krishna never goes even one step away from Vrindavan.
