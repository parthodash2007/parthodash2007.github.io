Vaishnava Pada Das Babaji Maharaj

Janmashtami is a sacred Vaishnava vrata commemorating the appearance of Lord Sri Krishna. Devotees observe this auspicious day with devotion, fasting, prayer, chanting and remembrance of the divine pastimes of Sri Krishna.

About Janmashtami

Janmashtami is observed on the appearance day of Sri Krishna. Devotees gather in temples and homes to hear Hari-katha, chant the holy names, offer prayers and remember the transcendental activities of the Lord.

How to Observe Janmashtami

Devotees traditionally spend the day in devotional activities. Depending on their health, circumstances and the guidance of their spiritual tradition, they may observe a fast and spend time in prayer, chanting and hearing about Sri Krishna.

  • Wake up early and maintain a devotional atmosphere.
  • Take a bath and wear clean clothes before beginning devotional activities.
  • Chant the holy names of Sri Krishna and engage in devotional prayers.
  • Read or hear descriptions of the appearance and pastimes of Sri Krishna.
  • Visit a temple or participate in Janmashtami celebrations when possible.
  • Offer suitable devotional offerings to Sri Krishna.
  • Participate in kirtana, bhajana and other devotional activities.

Fasting and Parana

The manner of fasting may vary according to the devotee's tradition, health and circumstances. Devotees should follow the instructions of their authorised spiritual teachers or local Vaishnava tradition regarding the type of fasting.

The fast should be broken, or parana performed, at the appropriate time according to the prescribed vrata calendar and local observance.

Things to Avoid

Janmashtami is intended to be observed in a devotional atmosphere. Devotees generally avoid activities that distract from remembrance of Sri Krishna.

  • Avoid unnecessary entertainment and distractions.
  • Avoid disrespectful behaviour toward devotees, spiritual teachers and sacred practices.
  • Avoid activities that interfere with the observance of the vrata.
  • Avoid breaking the fast before the prescribed parana time.

Devotional Activities

The day may be dedicated to hearing, chanting, remembering, worshipping and serving Sri Krishna. Devotees may also participate in kirtana, temple programs, scriptural reading and other devotional activities.

Conclusion

Janmashtami is an important opportunity for devotees to deepen their remembrance of Sri Krishna and increase their engagement in devotional service. The observance should be performed with sincerity, devotion and respect for the instructions of one's spiritual tradition.
