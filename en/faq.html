Frequently Asked Questions

About This Website

What is this website about?

This website is a spiritual and devotional resource for devotees of Sri Sri Radha-Krishna and the Vaishnava tradition. It provides access to religious texts, devotional teachings, Vaishnava observances, images, videos, and other spiritual resources.

Who is this website intended for?

The website is intended for devotees, spiritual seekers, and anyone interested in learning about Sri Sri Radha-Krishna, Vaishnava philosophy, devotional practices, and Hindu religious traditions.

Religious Texts

What religious texts can I read here?

The website may provide access to selected Hindu and Vaishnava scriptures, books, translations, commentaries, and other devotional literature.

Are all texts available in English and Bengali?

Availability may vary by text. We aim to provide resources in multiple languages where possible.

Vaishnava Observances

Where can I find the Vaishnava Vrata list?

The Vaishnava Vrata and observance calendar can be found in the Vrata List section of the website.

Are the dates of religious observances fixed?

Religious observance dates may vary according to the calendar, location, tradition, and local time. Please verify important dates with an appropriate traditional or authoritative source.
