01 Short Questions

QUESTION 01 Short Answer

What is a physical quantity?

Answer

A physical quantity is a measurable property that can be expressed by a numerical value and a unit.

QUESTION 02 Short Answer

What is a fundamental quantity?

Answer

A fundamental quantity is an independent physical quantity that is not expressed in terms of other physical quantities. Length, mass and time are examples.

QUESTION 03 Short Answer

What is a derived quantity?

Answer

A derived quantity is a physical quantity obtained from fundamental quantities. Velocity, acceleration and force are examples.

QUESTION 04 Short Answer

What is dimensional analysis?

Answer

Dimensional analysis is a method of studying physical relationships by using the dimensions of physical quantities.

02 Descriptive Questions

QUESTION 05 Descriptive

Explain the difference between fundamental and derived quantities with examples.

Answer

Fundamental quantities are independent physical quantities. They are not defined in terms of other physical quantities. Length, mass and time are examples of fundamental quantities.

Derived quantities are obtained by combining fundamental quantities through mathematical relationships. Velocity, acceleration and force are examples of derived quantities.

Fundamental Quantity Derived Quantity
Length Area
Mass Force
Time Velocity

03 Mathematical Problems

QUESTION 07 Problem Solving

Determine the dimensional formula of force.

Solution

We know that,

Force = Mass × Acceleration

The dimensional formula of mass is:

[M]

The dimensional formula of acceleration is:

[LT-2]

Therefore,

[F] = [M] × [LT-2]

Hence,

[F] = [MLT-2]

Answer: The dimensional formula of force is [MLT-2].

QUESTION 08 Problem Solving

Find the dimensional formula of work.

Solution

We know that,

Work = Force × Displacement

The dimensional formula of force is:

[MLT-2]

The dimensional formula of displacement is:

[L]

Therefore,

[W] = [MLT-2] × [L]
[W] = [ML2T-2]

Answer: The dimensional formula of work is [ML2T-2].

QUESTION 09 Problem Solving

Determine the dimensional formula of velocity.

Solution

We know that,

Velocity = Displacement / Time

Displacement has dimension [L] and time has dimension [T].

[v] = [L] / [T]
[v] = [LT-1]

Answer: The dimensional formula of velocity is [LT-1].
