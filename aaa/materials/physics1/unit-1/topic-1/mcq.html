QUESTION 01 Basic

Which of the following is a fundamental physical quantity?

Correct Answer: B — Mass

Mass is a fundamental quantity. Velocity, force and acceleration are derived quantities.

QUESTION 02 Basic

What is the SI unit of length?

Correct Answer: C — Metre

The metre (m) is the SI base unit of length.

QUESTION 03 Dimensions

What is the dimensional formula of velocity?

Correct Answer: A — [LT⁻¹]

Velocity = Displacement / Time, so its dimensional formula is [LT⁻¹].

QUESTION 04 Dimensions

What is the dimensional formula of force?

Correct Answer: D — [MLT⁻²]

Force = Mass × Acceleration. Therefore, [F] = [M] × [LT⁻²] = [MLT⁻²].

QUESTION 05 SI Units

What is the SI unit of electric current?

Correct Answer: B — Ampere

Ampere (A) is the SI base unit of electric current.

QUESTION 06 Dimensions

What is the dimensional formula of acceleration?

Correct Answer: C — [LT⁻²]

Acceleration = Change in velocity / Time. Its dimensional formula is [LT⁻²].

QUESTION 07 SI Units

How many SI base quantities are there?

Correct Answer: A — Seven

The SI system has seven base quantities.

QUESTION 08 Applications

Which of the following is a use of dimensional analysis?

Correct Answer: D — Checking dimensional consistency of equations

Dimensional analysis can help check whether the dimensions on both sides of an equation are consistent.
